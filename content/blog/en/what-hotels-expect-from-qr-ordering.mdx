Sunflow brings a hotel’s ordering and guest services together on one platform. My goal is to digitise hotels’ ordering processes and, by analysing guest behaviour, help businesses make better decisions.

Sunflow took second place in the [TourisTech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/touristech-incubation/) program. In this post I share what I care about in a QR ordering system for guests, staff and managers.

## Guests should order in their own language

Hotel guests come from many countries. Sunflow’s guest app works in five languages: Turkish, English, German, French and Russian.

Language does not stop at the menu:

- Notes that guests add to their order are translated for the staff.
- Staff can enter products in the language they prefer.

So even when guests and staff do not share a language, the order is understood correctly.

## Everyone should see their own screen

Managers and staff do not need the same things. Access in Sunflow is role-based: managers and staff see the screens they need for their own work.

## Guests should know what is happening

After ordering, a guest has one question: did my order go through? If the guest allows it, they get an instant notification when the order is received and when it is completed.

## Reports for managers

Managers do not have to open the panel every day. AI reports live in the panel and are also sent to managers by email, daily, weekly or monthly.

<Callout title="Note">
  Sunflow does not share guest data with third parties.
</Callout>

Sunflow is not a finished product. I add new modules based on what hotels need. First the need appears, then the module is built around it.
